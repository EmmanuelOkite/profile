KARIBU GROCERY LIMITED

Karibu Groceries
Limited (KGL)

Grocery Sales & Management Information System (GSMIS) is a scalable platform that streamlines grocery business operations from daily sales tracking to financial reporting. It integrates sales management, expense tracking, inventory monitoring, and loss control in one centralized system. Store owners can manage multiple products, monitor stock levels in real time, track profit margins, and generate automated reports for better decision-making. The platform supports role-based access, transaction history, and performance insights to improve accountability and efficiency.

ONESPAN CONSULTS LIMITED

Race Registration and
Management Information System (RMIS)

The Race Registration & Management Information System (RMIS) is a scalable platform that streamlines running event management from registration to post-race services. It integrates participant registration, payments, bib assignment, QR e-tickets, live results, and automated finisher certificates. Organizers can manage multiple events with custom branding, role-based access, and bulk communications. Participants access dashboards, photos, and race information in real time. The system reduces manual work, improves efficiency, and enhances the overall experience for organizers and runners. ↗

RORASERVICE APP

RoraService

RoraService is a citizen-government service bridge designed to close the digital access gap for people without smartphones in rural and peri-urban Uganda. It lets citizens submit requests, report issues, and reach the right government office using simple USSD codes on any feature phone, no internet or app required. Government offices receive these requests directly and can respond and resolve them without requiring an in-person visit. It gives government agencies clearer, real-time visibility into the issues communities are actually facing, while giving citizens a reliable way to be heard regardless of their device, location, or digital literacy. Built during a hackathon with Refactory Academy, the project addressed Uganda's Digital Governance challenge. ↗
